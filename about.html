About Me

From code to impact — my journey in tech

My journey into coding started with curiosity — the desire to understand how websites work and the power to create something from nothing. What began as a hobby quickly became a passion for building functional, user-friendly applications that solve real-world problems.

I'm currently pursuing my B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering at Dr. Akhilesh Das Gupta Institute of Professional Studies. My academic journey, combined with a Diploma in Computer Engineering from Guru Tegh Bahadur Polytechnic Institute, has given me a strong foundation in both theoretical and practical aspects of software development.

My technical toolkit spans across frontend and backend technologies — from HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to React, Node.js, and MongoDB. I'm constantly exploring new technologies and frameworks to expand my capabilities and build better solutions.

Beyond coding, I believe in holistic growth. I've won competitions, led debates, organized community campaigns, and spoken in front of hundreds. These experiences have shaped me into a well-rounded developer who values communication, teamwork, and making a positive impact.

🎯 Clean Code Philosophy

I believe in writing code that is clean, maintainable, and scalable. Good code should be readable by humans first, computers second.

🧠 Lifelong Learning

Technology evolves fast. I stay curious and continuously learn new technologies, frameworks, and best practices to stay ahead.

🤝 Collaboration First

The best solutions emerge through collaboration. I value teamwork, open communication, and learning from diverse perspectives.

🌍 Impact Driven

Technology should serve people. I'm driven by building solutions that make a real difference — from community campaigns to web applications.
